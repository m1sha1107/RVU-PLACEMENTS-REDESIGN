Industry-Ready Skills
Step One

Confirm your eligibility.

You may register for placement drives once every item below is satisfied.

No academic backlogs at the time of registering for placement drives, unless a recruiter permits otherwise.

Minimum 80% attendance in mandatory pre-placement training sessions.

Submission of the Placement Registration & Declaration Form.

Completion of required experiential components — internship, immersion or capstone — unless exceptionally approved by School leadership.

Compliance with all company-specific eligibility criteria.

No pending disciplinary case with the Student Disciplinary Committee (STDC).

Step Two

Complete pre-placement training.

Minimum 80% attendance is compulsory across all three components.

Good Practice

Your responsibilities as a candidate.

Placements work best when students, recruiters and CAR all hold up their end. Here's what's expected of you.

  • Apply selectively. Only to roles and organisations you're genuinely willing to join.

  • Stay professional. Maintain professional conduct at every stage of the process.

  • Honour your offer. Once selected, accepted offers are honoured in line with institutional ethics.

  • Speak up early. Report any exceptional concerns promptly to the CAR office.
