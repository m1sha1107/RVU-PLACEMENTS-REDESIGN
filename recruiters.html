How to Recruit

Set up a drive in five steps.

01

Share your JD

Send role details and eligibility criteria to the CAR team.

02

We shortlist & schedule

03

Pre-placement talk

Present your organisation and role directly to registered students.

04

Assessments

We coordinate logistics for tests, case studies and interview rounds.

05

Offer & onboarding
