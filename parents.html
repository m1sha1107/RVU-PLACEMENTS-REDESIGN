Behind the Scenes

How we support your child.

Mandatory training, not optional coaching

Every student completes domain, soft-skills and behavioural training — with 80% attendance required — before they're eligible to interview.

A dedicated coordination team

The Corporate & Alumni Relations (CAR) office manages every stage: scheduling, logistics, documentation and recruiter communication.

Clear eligibility rules for everyone

The same criteria — academic standing, attendance, conduct — apply consistently across every school and every drive.

A separate body for discipline

CAR runs the process, but never adjudicates disciplinary matters. Those go to the independent Student Disciplinary Committee (STDC).

Governance

The policy framework, in plain terms.

1
Coordination

CAR coordinates, it doesn't police

Corporate & Alumni Relations facilitates every placement and internship activity across schools — but stays out of disciplinary decisions.

2
Accountability

An independent disciplinary body

All placement-related violations are formally referred to the Student Disciplinary Committee (STDC) under the University's Code of Conduct.

3
Consistency

One eligibility bar for everyone

No backlogs, 80% training attendance, a signed declaration form and no pending disciplinary case — the same rules, every school.

4
Ethics

Offers are meant to be honoured

Once a student accepts an offer, they're expected to honour it — in line with institutional ethics and industry norms.
